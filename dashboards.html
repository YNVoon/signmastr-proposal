Personal Performance Dashboard

Available to All Team Members: Each employee has access to a personal performance dashboard showing their quarterly scoring and bonus tracking.

Current Quarter Summary

📊 Total Points

Total points accumulated this quarter

  • Individual Performance (50%)
  • Collaboration Quality (30%)
  • Customer Impact (20%)

💰 Projected Bonus

Projected bonus based on current points

  • Current performance tier
  • Bonus multiplier
  • Estimated payout

📈 Performance Level

Current tier: Developing to Exceptional

  • Progress to next tier
  • Points needed for advancement
  • Tier requirements

⭐ Recent Activity

Latest points earned

  • Last 10 point awards
  • Recent projects completed
  • Recent peer ratings

Performance Insights

🤝 Collaboration Ratings

How others rated your handoffs and teamwork

💡 Areas for Improvement

Metrics where additional points could be earned

🌟 Strengths

Areas where you consistently earn full points

📜 Historical Performance

Previous quarters' scores and trends

No Competition Features: Personal dashboards show only your own performance - no leaderboards, no comparison to colleagues, no public rankings. Focus is on personal improvement only.
