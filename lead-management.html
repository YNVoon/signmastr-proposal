Lead Management & Sales

Comprehensive lead capture, tracking, and conversion system integrated with WhatsApp Business and website forms.

Lead Sources

📱 WhatsApp Business

Primary Source

Leads generated from Facebook ads directed to WhatsApp Business

  • Multiple WhatsApp Business numbers supported
  • Each number can be assigned to specific salespeople
  • Automatic lead creation from new conversations
  • Full conversation history stored in system

🌐 Website SEO Forms

Secondary Source

Organic leads from website contact forms

  • Automatic form submission capture
  • Lead data mapped to system fields
  • Email notifications to sales team
  • Response tracking and analytics

Lead Intake Process

Step 1: Lead Arrives

Lead comes in from WhatsApp Business or website form

Step 2: Automatic Lead Creation

System automatically creates a new lead record with all available information

Step 3: Salesperson Assignment

System assigns or suggests salesperson based on:

  • WhatsApp Business number used (if applicable)
  • Round-robin distribution
  • Manual assignment by administrator

Step 4: Notification

Assigned salesperson receives notification and can begin engagement

WhatsApp Integration Details

Dual System Support

The system supports both WhatsApp Business app and WhatsApp API simultaneously, allowing flexible transition from existing workflows to new system-based workflows.

Key Features

Multiple Numbers

Handle multiple WhatsApp Business numbers within the system

Number Assignment

Each number can be assigned to specific salesperson(s)

Conversation Visibility

All WhatsApp conversations visible within the system interface

Flexible Response

Sales team can choose to respond via WhatsApp Business app (existing workflow) or new system interface (future workflow)

Response Options

  • Option 1 (Current): Continue using WhatsApp Business app for responses while system tracks conversations
  • Option 2 (Future): Respond directly through the system interface with full WhatsApp integration
