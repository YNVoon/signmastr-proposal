Manufacturing Process

Production planning, material management, quality control, and work order tracking for custom signboard manufacturing.

Manufacturing Overview

Given the highly customizable nature of signboard manufacturing, the system supports comprehensive job order management, material tracking, and quality control processes.

Track common materials, components, and inventory levels

Prioritize jobs by deadline, materials, and complexity

Quality checks, photo documentation, and verification

Suggested Manufacturing Workflow

1. Approved Design → Generate Job Order

System automatically creates manufacturing job order from approved design

2. Review Materials Needed → Check Inventory

Review required materials and check inventory levels

3. Material Availability Check

If materials available: Proceed to production

If materials needed: Order/requisition materials

4. Manufacturing Team Selects Job

Team member selects job from queue based on priority

5. Mark Job as "In Progress"

Update status and begin production work

6. Complete Work with Photo Documentation

Finish production and upload photos during process

7. Quality Check with Verification Photos

Complete quality checklist and upload verification photos

8. Mark as "Ready for Installation"

Job passes quality check and is ready for installer

9. Notify Administration

System notifies administrator for installation scheduling
