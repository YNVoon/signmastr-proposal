Welcome to Signmastr Business Management System

A comprehensive business management system designed to streamline operations from initial customer contact through to project completion and invoicing.

Version: 1.0 | Date: October 22, 2025 | Status: Draft for Review

Executive Summary

Purpose

This system outlines the requirements for Signmastr's comprehensive business management platform. The system will streamline operations from initial customer contact through to project completion and invoicing, integrating lead handling, sales, design, manufacturing, and installation processes.

Business Objectives

Centralization

Centralize all customer interactions and project information in one unified system

Lead Tracking

Improve lead tracking and conversion rates through automated workflows

Collaboration

Enhance team collaboration across all departments

Automation

Automate administrative workflows to reduce manual work

Visibility

Provide real-time visibility into project status for all stakeholders

Performance

Track performance metrics for sales and operations

System Overview

Complete Project Lifecycle

1. Lead Capture

Automatic lead creation from WhatsApp Business and website forms

2. Sales & Site Visit

Lead assignment, customer communication, site visit documentation

3. Basic Draft & Quotation

Designer creates basic layout, salesperson prepares quotation

4. Quotation Approval

Administrator reviews and approves quotation before sending to customer

5. Payment Collection

50% upfront payment triggers full design phase

6. Full Design Phase

Detailed design creation, customer approval, version control

7. Manufacturing

Production planning, quality control, material management

8. Installation

Installer assignment, delivery, customer sign-off

9. Project Closure

Final invoice, payment collection, project archival

Key Features

🔄 WhatsApp Integration

Direct integration with multiple WhatsApp Business numbers for seamless lead capture and customer communication

📊 Performance Tracking

Comprehensive quarterly scoring and bonus system to measure and reward individual performance

👥 Role-Based Access

Six distinct user roles with customized dashboards and permissions

📱 Mobile App

Dedicated installer mobile app for site check-in/out, photo documentation, and digital signatures

🎨 Design Management

Complete design workflow with version control, amendment tracking, and approval processes

🏭 Manufacturing Control

Job order management, material tracking, quality control, and production planning

💰 Autocount Integration

Automatic generation of quotations, invoices, and delivery orders through Autocount API

📈 Real-time Analytics

Comprehensive dashboards and reports for all roles and business metrics

Success Criteria

Business Metrics

  • 100% of leads captured in the system
  • Quotation approval time reduced by 50%
  • Design revision cycles tracked and optimized
  • Installation documentation 100% complete
  • Invoice generation time reduced by 70%
  • Visibility into project status for all stakeholders

User Adoption

  • Sales team actively using system for lead management within 30 days
  • Design team using portal for all projects within 30 days
  • Installers using mobile app for all jobs within 30 days
  • Administrator managing all approvals through system

Process Improvements

  • Clear audit trail for all projects
  • Reduced manual data entry
  • Faster communication between departments
  • Fewer lost leads or forgotten follow-ups
  • Better resource allocation
