Performance Scoring & Bonus System

Comprehensive quarterly scoring system designed to measure and reward individual performance based on measurable outcomes.

Overview

Purpose: Measure individual performance and award quarterly bonuses based on measurable outcomes, while promoting teamwork and quality work.

Key Principles

📅 Quarterly Cycle

Scoring periods run every 3 months

🎓 30-Day Learning Period

First 30 days are practice mode - scores visible but not counted toward bonuses

➕ Positive Reinforcement Only

Good performance earns points, poor performance simply doesn't award points

👥 Dynamic Teams

Each project involves different team members, individual contributions tracked per project

🔍 Full Transparency

All scoring rules visible to employees with personal dashboards

🚫 No Competition

No leaderboards or public rankings - focus on personal performance

Complexity Multipliers

To ensure fairness across different project types, complexity multipliers are applied to base job completion points:

Complexity Level Multiplier Description
Simple 1.0x Standard signboard designs, minimal customization, standard materials
Medium 1.5x Custom designs, LED integration, multiple components, special materials
High 2.0x Fully custom unique designs, complex LED/electrical work, large-scale installations, multiple locations, special engineering

Assigned by: Administrator when project enters system

Quarterly Bonus Calculation

Note: Point tiers will be calibrated after learning period based on actual performance data

Point Tiers & Bonus Structure

Total Points Performance Level Bonus Multiplier
0 - 2,000 Developing 0.5x base
2,001 - 4,000 Meeting Expectations 1.0x base
4,001 - 6,000 Exceeding Expectations 1.5x base
6,001 - 8,000 Outstanding 2.0x base
8,001+ Exceptional 2.5x base

Calculation Example

Quarterly base bonus pool: RM 500 per eligible employee

Employee scores: 6,500 points (Outstanding level)

Bonus calculation: RM 500 × 2.0 = RM 1,000

Quality Safeguards

Prevents Speed Without Quality: The system includes automatic quality gates to ensure speed bonuses are only awarded when quality standards are met. 
IF speed bonus conditions met AND quality conditions met:
  AWARD speed bonus + quality bonus
ELSE IF only speed conditions met:
  AWARD 0 points (quality standards not met)
ELSE IF only quality conditions met:
  AWARD quality bonus only

Examples

  • Designer completes in 2 days BUT requires 3 revisions → No speed bonus
  • Manufacturer completes 30% faster BUT fails quality check → No speed bonus
  • Installer completes ahead of schedule AND customer gives 5 stars → Full bonuses awarded

Learning Period (First 30 Days)

✅ System Active

Full system active and visible during learning period

📊 Points Calculated

Points calculated and displayed in real-time

🎓 Learning Mode

Points DO NOT count toward bonuses during learning period

🚀 After Learning

Points start counting toward quarterly bonus after 30 days

Allows everyone to understand the system without pressure. Training and support provided throughout learning period.

Fair Scoring with Dynamic Teams

How System Ensures Fairness

1. Individual Control

Your score is primarily based on your own work quality and speed

2. Independent Collaboration

Your handoff quality is rated by the next person, regardless of who they are

3. Shared Customer Success

Everyone benefits when customer is happy (team effort)

4. No Penalty for Others

If another department delays, you don't lose your earned points

Example Scenario

Designer completes job quickly and well

✅ Earns speed + quality points

Manufacturing takes longer than expected

✅ Designer not penalized, keeps individual points

Designer's handoff was clean

✅ Earns collaboration points

Customer gives 5 stars

✅ Everyone shares +100 points customer impact bonus

Result

You control your score through your work quality, while still benefiting from team success

Administration & Dispute Resolution

Administrator Controls

  • Assign and adjust complexity multipliers
  • Review and correct scoring errors
  • Handle disputes or questions
  • Adjust point tiers after quarterly calibration
  • Generate reports on scoring trends
  • Pause scoring for extraordinary circumstances

Appeals Process

1. Contact Administrator

Within 5 days of quarter end, with specific project details

2. Administrator Review

Reviews with supporting evidence

3. Correction if Valid

Made within 7-day review period

4. Employee Notified

Of outcome and any adjustments

Common Valid Corrections

  • Missed points due to system error
  • Incorrect complexity rating applied
  • Wrong peer rating assigned to wrong person
  • Documentation uploaded but not recorded
  • System downtime prevented timely action

Quarterly Calibration

After Each Quarter, Administrators Will:

1. Review Distribution

How many employees in each tier? Are tiers appropriately challenging?

2. Adjust Tiers If Needed

If 80% in one tier, adjust thresholds for fairness

3. Gather Feedback

Survey employees on system fairness and identify any issues

4. Communicate Changes

Announce adjustments before new quarter with clear rationale
